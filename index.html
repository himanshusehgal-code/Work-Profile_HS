About

I specialize in business process automation, MIS, data management and AI-enabled workflows . My work combines practical business understanding with technical implementation using Google Sheets, Apps Script, Excel, SQL, Python, APIs and AI technologies.

My approach is not limited to using pre-built automation templates. I focus on understanding the actual business problem, designing the workflow, structuring the data and building the technical solution required to solve it.

I work at the intersection of ground-level execution and management decision-making . I understand how staff actually perform processes and translate operational problems into structured systems that provide managers with visibility, control, reporting and accountability.

I have experience working with staff, supervisors, managers and business stakeholders to convert repetitive manual activities into automated workflows, dashboards, reporting systems, communication systems and custom business applications.

My current development focus is AI-powered business automation . I am learning to integrate AI directly into software workflows for information extraction, classification, document processing, decision support, reporting and intelligent business assistance.

AI Automation Approach

I focus on using AI as a component inside real software systems — not simply using a chatbot or copying a pre-built automation template.

Business Problem → Data / Input → Python / Apps Script → AI Processing → SQL / Validation → Business Action

From Development to Deployment

My goal is to take an automation from an idea and local development environment to a working, accessible application.

Problem → Python → SQL / API → AI → GitHub → Render → Working Application
